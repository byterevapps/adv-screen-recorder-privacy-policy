Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026/11/25

This Privacy Policy explains what information the ADV Screen Recorder app (the "App") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

In short: your recordings stay on your device and we never see them. The App sends crash reports and, if you agree, usage statistics to Google Firebase. The App shows no ads, doesn't use your advertising ID, and we never sell your data.

1. Who We Are

The App is provided by the developer named on its Google Play page ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the information described in this policy. Our full name, address and phone number are shown with the developer details on that page.

Email: byterevapps@gmail.com

2. Your Recordings

Recordings are saved only to the storage location you choose on your device. The App doesn't upload them, and we can't see them.

Your screen, internal audio, microphone and camera are only captured while you are recording, and only to create the recording on your device. Images and text you add as overlays, and edits such as trimming a video, also stay on your device.

3. Permissions the App Uses

  • Screen capture (Android asks you each time you start a recording): to record your screen and, on Android 10 or newer, internal audio.
  • Microphone: to record your voice or the sound around you, and because Android also requires it to record internal audio.
  • Camera: only when you turn on the camera overlay, to show your camera on screen while you record.
  • Display over other apps: to show the floating recording controls and overlays.
  • Photos, videos and storage: to save your recordings and show them in the App.
  • Notifications: to show the recording notification and its controls.
  • Run in the background and ignore battery optimization: to keep recording while you use other apps, without Android stopping the recording to save battery.
  • Modify system settings: to turn Android's "Show touches" option on and off for you, where Android allows it.
  • Internet access and Google Play billing: to send crash reports and usage statistics, and to let you buy Pro.

4. Information Collected Automatically

a. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When the App crashes or runs into an error, it sends a report to help us find and fix the problem. A report includes your device model and manufacturer, Android version, App version, random installation identifiers, technical details of the error, and the App's state at the time: recording settings (such as resolution, bitrate, frame rate, orientation, audio mode, recording engine, video codec, and whether the camera or countdown was used), battery optimization status and your choices in the App's battery prompts, the type of storage used, and the location of the recording file being saved, which can include the names of folders you chose. Crash reports never include the content of your recordings.

Crash reports are sent from the moment you install the App, because keeping the App working is in our legitimate interest. You can object to this by contacting us.

b. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). Only collected after you accept this Privacy Policy in the App. They include a random app instance identifier (not your advertising ID), when you open the App and for how long, which screens you open, which features and settings you use, your device model, Android version and language, how the App was installed, and your approximate location (country or city), which Google derives from your IP address. We use them to understand which features matter to you and which devices to test on. They are not used for advertising.

c. Purchases. Google Play processes purchases of Pro. Google Play gives us order details such as the order number, product, price and date, so we can manage purchases and refunds. We never receive your payment details.

d. Emails. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

5. Who We Share Information With

We use Google as our service provider for crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and purchases (Google Play). Google processes this information on our behalf. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google may process this information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We don't share your information with anyone else, and we never sell it.

6. How Long We Keep Information

  • Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Usage statistics linked to an app instance identifier are deleted automatically after the retention period set in our Google Analytics settings, which is never longer than 14 months. Aggregated reports that can't identify you may be kept longer.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play, and by us for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Emails are kept for as long as needed to answer you.

7. How We Protect Information

Crash reports and usage statistics are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on Google's servers, which are protected by Google's security measures. Only we have access to this information in our Firebase account.

8. Your Choices and Rights

We don't keep any user accounts or database. Crash reports and usage statistics are stored in our Firebase account with random identifiers only, not your name or email address, so we can't tell which of them belong to you. They are deleted automatically (see section 6), and uninstalling the App stops all collection.

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent. Because we can't identify you from crash reports or usage statistics, these rights apply to them only if you give us details that let us find your data. For emails you've sent us or questions about a purchase, email us and we'll help. Your purchase records are also held by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Portugal this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (www.cnpd.pt), or you can contact the authority in the country where you live.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or the law changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, the App will ask you to review and accept the new version.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at byterevapps@gmail.com.
